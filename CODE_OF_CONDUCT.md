# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The **TEJAS-MK2 Calculator** project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for users, contributors, maintainers, package consumers, and community members.

We want technical discussions, bug reports, code reviews, and package contributions to remain professional and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, patience, and professionalism.
- Communicate constructively, including when disagreeing.
- Welcome good-faith questions, different perspectives, and constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, ideas, or decisions rather than people.
- Give appropriate credit to contributors and respect licenses.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work toward constructive resolution.
- Respect project boundaries, maintainer decisions, and moderation actions.
- Avoid unnecessary disruption of repository, package, or community spaces.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or derogatory comments targeting individuals or groups.
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual harassment.
- Trolling, intimidation, doxxing, or deliberate disruption.
- Repeated bad-faith participation intended to derail productive discussion.
- Publishing private or personal information without permission.
- Impersonating contributors, maintainers, package owners, or project representatives.
- Malicious behavior intended to harm the project, its users, its packages, or its infrastructure.
- Deliberate abuse of package registries, CI systems, or project automation.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Project Spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository and project-managed spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, comments, documentation, package-related discussions, and project communication channels.

It also applies when someone is representing the project or its community in a public setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or project infrastructure.

Possible actions include:

1. A private or public warning when appropriate.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of content that violates this Code of Conduct.
4. Temporary or permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement should be fair, proportionate, and focused on maintaining a safe and productive community.

## Reporting a Problem

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately through the repository's available maintainer, moderation, or reporting channel.

When possible, include:

- What happened.
- When and where it happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or context.
- Usernames or project accounts involved.

Do not publicly expose private information or sensitive evidence about anyone involved in a report.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) instead of posting technical details publicly.

## Scope of Enforcement

Maintainers may act when conduct is clearly connected to the project, its contributors, or its community, including conduct outside the repository when someone is acting in an official project capacity or when the conduct has a direct and substantial impact on the project community.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers should:

- Apply these standards consistently and in good faith.
- Avoid unnecessary public disclosure of incident details.
- Give participants an opportunity to provide relevant context when practical.
- Keep enforcement focused on protecting people and maintaining a productive project.
- Avoid using moderation powers for personal disputes or retaliation.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the principles and structure of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), with project-specific language for this repository.

## Questions

For general questions, open a GitHub issue. For sensitive conduct reports or security matters, use the repository's private reporting channels.
