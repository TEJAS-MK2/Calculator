# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The **TEJAS-MK2 Calculator** project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for contributors, users, maintainers, and community members.

Participation in this project is a privilege that comes with the responsibility to communicate professionally and help maintain a healthy open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat everyone with respect, patience, and professionalism.
- Communicate constructively, especially when disagreeing.
- Welcome good-faith questions, different perspectives, and constructive feedback.
- Focus technical criticism on code, ideas, documentation, or decisions rather than people.
- Give credit where it is due and respect the work of other contributors.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work toward resolving problems constructively.
- Respect project boundaries, community guidelines, and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or derogatory comments targeting individuals or groups.
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or sexual harassment.
- Trolling, intimidation, doxxing, or deliberate disruption of project spaces.
- Repeated bad-faith participation intended to derail productive discussion.
- Publishing private or personal information without permission.
- Impersonating contributors, maintainers, or project representatives.
- Malicious behavior intended to harm the project, its contributors, or its users.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Project Spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository and all project-managed spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, comments, and project communication channels.

It also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in a public setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Possible enforcement actions include:

1. A private or public warning, when appropriate.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of content that violates this Code of Conduct.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement will be handled fairly and proportionately, taking the circumstances and severity of the incident into account.

## Reporting a Problem

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the repository's available contact or reporting channels.

When possible, include:

- What happened.
- When and where it happened.
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant context.
- The usernames or project accounts involved.

Please avoid publicly sharing sensitive or private information about anyone involved in a report.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope of Enforcement

Maintainers may take action when conduct is clearly connected to the project, its contributors, or its community, including conduct outside the repository when someone is acting in an official project capacity or when the conduct has a direct and substantial impact on the project community.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers should:

- Apply these standards consistently and in good faith.
- Avoid unnecessary public disclosure of incident details.
- Give participants an opportunity to provide relevant context when practical.
- Keep enforcement focused on maintaining a safe and productive project environment.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the principles and structure of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), with project-specific language for this repository.

## Questions

For general questions about this Code of Conduct, open a GitHub issue. For sensitive matters or incident reports, use the repository's private reporting/contact channel instead.
